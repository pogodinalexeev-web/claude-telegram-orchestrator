# ideas — take-or-not

The "far layer": things to decide on, reviewed weekly (not mixed into the daily
brief). An idea graduates into a real project (via the `/graduate` skill) once there's
a loop for it or the owner says "let's do it".

- <an idea waiting for a decision> #soon YYYY-MM-DD
- <a pointer to journal material> — [journal/<file>.md](path)

> Rule: `#later` / `#soon` tags must carry a date or a clear trigger. A bare tag with
> no condition is procrastination in disguise and gets culled at weekly review.
